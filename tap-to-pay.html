The Approach

Being part of a new product launch meant our team at Square had the unique challenge of building trust from scratch, particularly in the US, where tap-to-pay is still emerging.

My Role

  • Led Content Strategy
  • UX Writing
  • Content Design

The challenge was clear: make an invisible technology feel tangible, trustworthy, and effortless.

Understanding Our Audience

New Sellers

First-time business owners unfamiliar with payment processing. They need confidence-building language that demystifies the technology and clear onboarding that reduces anxiety.

Existing Sellers

Experienced merchants already using Square hardware. They want efficiency-focused content that highlights speed and flexibility without redundant explanations.

The User Journey

Step 01

A new way to take payments

Introducing contactless payments without additional hardware.

Step 02

All payment types welcome

Support for credit, debit, and digital wallets in one tap.

Step 03

Hold and wait for the tone

Clear audio and visual feedback guides the interaction.

Step 04

Get ready to give it a go

Onboarding that builds confidence from the first transaction.
